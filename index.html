About the NASA Data

Every number in this game comes from real NASA mission data, not invented statistics.

NASA DONKI — Space Weather Database
Solar flare events (class, magnitude, CME speed) are drawn from the DONKI archive at api.nasa.gov. The game attempts a live fetch each session. If unavailable it uses a curated set of 15 real historical events from 2023–2024, including the major X3.9 flare on May 10, 2024.
Curiosity RAD — Mars Surface Radiation
The baseline Martian surface radiation of 0.64 mSv per sol comes from the RAD instrument aboard Curiosity. Source: Hassler et al. (2014), Science 343. This is the first radiation measurement made on another planet's surface.
NASA LRO — Lunar South Pole
Lunar temperature ranges (+20 to -240 C), Peaks of Eternal Light, and Permanently Shadowed Region data come from LRO datasets published by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov).
Perseverance MEDA — Mars Environmental Data
Surface temperature ranges at Jezero Crater (-15 C day, -80 C night), 95% CO2 atmosphere, and ~650 Pa pressure from the MEDA instrument, available via NASA's Planetary Data System.
NASA OSDR — OSD-1018 Crew Isolation Study
The crew readiness penalty for extended shelter stays is informed by OSD-1018, which studied the combined effects of space radiation and social isolation on behavior. Available at osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo.
Communication Delay
Lunar delay (1.3 sec one-way) and Mars delay (4–24 min) are based on actual Earth-Moon and Earth-Mars distances at the speed of light.
